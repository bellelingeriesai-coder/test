Logistics & Support

Shipping Policy

At Tweens Global by Belle Lingeries, we are committed to ensuring smooth and reliable shipment coordination for our international buyers and business partners. Our shipping process is designed to support efficient order handling, export compliance, and timely dispatch.

1. Order Processing

Orders are processed after confirmation of product specifications, quantities, packaging details, and agreed payment terms. Production timelines may vary depending on order size and customization requirements.

2. Shipping Methods

We support international shipments through air freight, sea freight, and other logistics solutions based on buyer requirements, order volume, destination country, and urgency.

3. Delivery Timelines

Estimated shipment timelines are shared during order confirmation. Delivery schedules may vary depending on production timelines, customs clearance, shipping carrier schedules, and destination country regulations.

4. Export Documentation

Necessary export-related documents such as commercial invoices, packing lists, and shipping documentation will be prepared as per agreed shipment terms and applicable export requirements.

5. Packaging Standards

Products are packed using export-suitable packaging methods to help ensure product safety during transportation and handling. Customized packaging requirements may also be supported upon request.

6. Shipping Charges

Shipping costs, freight charges, insurance, duties, taxes, and import-related expenses shall be handled as per mutually agreed trade terms between the buyer and Tweens Global by Belle Lingeries.

7. Customs & Import Duties

Buyers are responsible for complying with local import regulations, duties, taxes, and customs clearance procedures applicable in their respective countries.

8. Shipment Tracking

Shipment and dispatch details will be shared with buyers whenever applicable to support shipment tracking and coordination.

9. Delays & External Factors

While we strive to maintain timely shipments, delays caused by customs procedures, port congestion, transportation disruptions, force majeure events, or external logistics factors may occur beyond our direct control.

10. Damaged or Missing Shipments

Any concerns related to shipment damage, shortages, or delivery discrepancies should be reported promptly with supporting documentation and photographs, where applicable, to assist in review and resolution.

11. International Business Support

Our team works closely with buyers, distributors, and logistics partners to support smooth export coordination and long-term international business relationships.

Need Assistance with Orders or Bulk Shipments?

Whether you need real-time shipping updates, commercial logistics coordinates, or custom dispatch schedules for wholesale orders—our support team is at your service.
