Privacy Policy

At Tweens by Belle Lingeries, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, enquire about our bonded lingerie products, or partner with us for retail, distribution, or private label services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out contact and business enquiry forms, subscribe to our newsletters, or communicate with our sales team. This includes your name, company name, phone number, email address, country, and specific requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to process and respond to your business and product enquiries, facilitate sampling and order coordination, provide customer service, send marketing communications (where permitted), and improve our website performance and user experience.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share relevant data with trusted third-party service providers (such as logistics partners, IT suppliers, and payment processors) who assist us in operating our business and delivering our products to you.

4. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures, including physical, administrative, and technological safeguards, to maintain the safety of your personal information when you submit enquiries or access data through our website.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance navigation, analyze site traffic, and understand user preferences. You can configure your browser settings to refuse cookies, though some features of the site may not function fully as a result.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as our social media pages or retail stores). We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of these external websites, and encourage you to review their privacy policies directly.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying any legal, accounting, reporting, or operational business requirements.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or limit the processing of your information. Please contact our data compliance team for any such requests.

9. International Data Transfers

As an international lingerie manufacturer and exporter, personal information we collect may be transferred, stored, and processed in India or any other country where our business operations or service providers are located.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operational, legal, or regulatory practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us at manvi@bellelingeries.com.
