Frequently Asked Questions

1. What products does Tweens by Belle Lingeries specialize in?

Tweens by Belle Lingeries specializes in women’s intimatewear including seamless bras, bonded bras, T-shirt bras, wire-free bras, everyday essentials, and comfort-focused innerwear collections designed for modern international markets.

2. Do you offer private label or OEM manufacturing?

Yes. We support private label and OEM manufacturing for distributors, retailers, importers, and lingerie brands. We can customize branding, labels, packaging, colors, and selected product specifications based on your market requirements.

3. What makes Tweens Global different from other lingerie suppliers?

Our focus is on combining premium product aesthetics, modern bonded technology, comfort-driven design, and reliable manufacturing while maintaining commercially competitive pricing. We prioritize fit consistency, clean finishing, and long-term buyer relationships.

4. Can you customize products for different countries or market preferences?

Yes. Different markets have different preferences in sizing, coverage, colors, padding, fabrics, and fit. Our team can help develop collections tailored to your local consumer demand and retail positioning.

5. What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the product category, customization level, and packaging requirements. We work with buyers to suggest practical order structures that support both business scalability and efficient production.

6. Do you provide samples before bulk production?

Yes. We provide samples for quality evaluation, fit approval, and product testing before finalizing bulk production. This helps ensure the product aligns with your expectations and market needs.

7. How do you maintain product quality and fit consistency?

Our production process includes quality checks across fabric inspection, cutting, bonding, stitching, finishing, and packing stages. We also conduct fit evaluations and technical checks to maintain consistency across sizes and production batches.

8. What is bonded technology and why is it important?

Bonded technology uses advanced adhesive techniques instead of traditional stitching in selected garment areas. This creates smoother finishes, reduced seam visibility, lightweight comfort, and a more modern premium look preferred by today’s consumers.

9. Which international markets do you currently cater to?

Tweens Global by Belle Lingeries is focused on expanding across global markets including Africa, the Middle East, and other international regions through export-oriented manufacturing and product development.

10. How can we start working with Tweens Global by Belle Lingeries?

You can contact our team to discuss your market requirements, target price range, preferred product categories, sampling process, and order plans. We would be happy to explore a long-term partnership opportunity with your business.
